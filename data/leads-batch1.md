## Methodology Notes & Caveats
- All businesses were found via Google Maps local search (browser automation; results scrolled to ~20-80 per query across plumbing, HVAC, roofing, handyman, landscaping, cleaning, junk removal searches in Austin, Denver, Phoenix, Charlotte). "No website" = no website link on the business's Google Maps card; a small share could have an unlinked site — outreach should confirm.
- Website audits were done by fetching each homepage and checking: mobile viewport meta, tel: link, contact form, booking/quote language, generator meta, and content-age markers.
- Emails were taken from published mailto: links on each site or listing; none were guessed.
- **Suggested outreach priority:** no-website leads (#1-3, 16-18, 24, 28-29) → broken/placeholder (#19-20, 25, 13) → template/DIY (#5, 26) → outdated sites (rest) using the Gap Scan angle ("we already found X problems on your site").
- Raw per-query extraction files (name/phone/website) are saved under `/tmp/*.json` on the work machine for re-verification.
