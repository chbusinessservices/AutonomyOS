## 1. Opening Call Script (30–60 seconds)

**The core principle: we're not selling anything in this call. We're pointing at a real problem they already have and offering a free, zero-risk way to see it on paper.** The Gap Scan is genuinely free, and the call only has one goal: **get them to say yes to the scan** (or to a 10-minute conversation about it). Never pitch price or packages on the first call.

### 1a. Master script (cold call, first attempt)

> "Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] with CH Business Services — I'm not trying to sell you anything, I promise. I was looking at [Business Name] in [City] because I help local service companies, and I noticed you don't have a website [**or:** your website isn't showing up right / is basically a placeholder / isn't working]. And honestly? For a [plumbing company / HVAC company / handyman service] in [City], that's a problem — because when someone's water heater dies, the first thing they do is Google '[plumbing company near me].' If there's nothing to find, the job goes to whoever has a website.
>
> So here's what I'd like to do — completely free, no strings, no sales pitch: I'll run a quick **Business Gap Scan** on your business. It takes me about 15 minutes, and it shows you exactly what customers see when they look you up — and what the competition is doing that's pulling those jobs away from you. You get the report either way, whether we ever work together or not.
>
> If I send that over, would you take a quick look at it?"

### 1b. Broken-website variation (use when the lead HAS a site that's broken/placeholder/down)

### 1c. Facebook-only variation (Plumb Masters)

## 2. Voicemail Script (15–20 seconds)

**Different from the live pitch — needs a hook. Curiosity, not a pitch. Never leave a salesy voicemail; leave a reason to call back.**

> "Hey [First Name], it's [Your Name] with CH Business Services. I was doing a quick look at [City] [niche] businesses and noticed [Business Name] doesn't show up when people search online — and I found something about how customers find you that I think you'll want to see. No sales pitch, it's a free report. Give me a call back at [phone] — it's [Your Name], and the number again is [phone]. Thanks!"

**Hook options (pick the strongest for the lead):**
- No-website lead: *"...noticed [Business Name] doesn't show up when people search for a plumber in Austin — and I found something about how customers find you..."*
- Broken-site lead: *"...your website at [domain] isn't loading right now — and I spotted it before a customer did. There's a fix that takes 15 minutes and costs nothing. Call me back..."*
- Website-down lead (J&R): *"...your website is down right now — if a customer tries to find you today, they can't. I can get you a free look at the fix. Call me back..."*

**Why it works:** it references their business specifically (they know it's not a robocall), promises a free deliverable, and creates a tiny mystery ("something I found"). 15 seconds max, one callback number.

**After a "not interested" or "too busy":**

## 5. Call Sequencing Strategy

### Cadence overview (14-attempt max per lead over ~4 weeks)

| Attempt | Day / Window | Channel | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1, Tue–Thu, 10:00–11:30am or 4:00–5:30pm local | Call | Live pitch (Section 1). No answer → voicemail (Section 2) + SMS (Section 3) within 30 min |
| 2 | Day 3 (2 days later), same windows | Call | Reconnect: "following up on the voicemail" angle |
| 3 | Day 5 | Call + SMS | Last call of week 1; SMS with link if no contact |
| 4 | Day 8 (Week 2, Tue) | Call | New week, fresh energy: "checking in before I close the file" |
| 5 | Day 10 | Call | Different time window — try opposite of earlier attempts (e.g., if you called mornings, try 4:30pm) |
| 6 | Day 12 | SMS only | "Last one from me — link here, no pressure" |
| 7 | Day 15 (Week 3) | Call | Final call attempt: "one last try — happy to close the file if you're all set" |
| — | Day 16 | — | **Give up on calls.** One LinkedIn connect + note (if profile found), then one LinkedIn follow-up 7 days later. |
| 14 | Day ~23 | LinkedIn | Final follow-up; then close the lead. Recycle back to the pool in 60–90 days. |

### Best days & times for SMB owners (service trades)
- **Best days:** **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.** Mondays are catching-up/emergency chaos; Fridays are wrap-up (and Friday afternoons are dead — no one picks up).
- **Best windows:** **10:00–11:30am** (after the morning rush, before lunch jobs) and **4:00–5:30pm** (between jobs, wrapping the day, back in the truck). Avoid 12–2pm (lunch) and before 9am (on jobs).
- **For tradespeople specifically:** they work *when the phone rings* — owners of one-to-five-person shops often answer any time, but they talk longer in the 4–5pm window.
- **Saturdays:** plumbing/HVAC/emergency trades often work Saturdays — a Saturday 10am call to a no-website plumber can catch the owner sitting in the truck between calls. Use sparingly (only on attempt 4+).
- **Time zones:** all leads are US local (Austin CT, Denver MT, Phoenix MT, Charlotte ET). Respect the lead's local time, not yours.

### How many attempts & when to give up
- **7 call attempts max** (3 weeks), then switch fully to LinkedIn (1 note + 1 follow-up). Total human touches: ~14 max over ~4 weeks.
- **Give up on calls** after attempt 7 with zero contact. **Give up on LinkedIn** after the second follow-up (day ~23).
- **Exceptions — escalate, don't quit:**
  - **J & R Junk Removal (#25, website DOWN):** their site is dead *right now* — a paying customer today can't find them. This is a "whenever you can, call twice a day" hot lead for 5 days, then fall into normal cadence.
  - **Any lead who says "call me back later/tomorrow":** honor it — set a precise follow-up time and call exactly then. Write it down; nothing loses a deal faster than missing a promised callback.
- **Switch channels when:** 3 unanswered calls → add SMS. 5 unanswered → vary the time window. Voicemail left + SMS sent + 2 calls → try LinkedIn.

### Pipeline rules
- After a **yes to the Gap Scan:** log it, send the scan same-day, and schedule a 10-min call 48h later to walk through findings → hand to Negotiator/closer pipeline.
- After a **"no"**: one polite SMS close-out (Section 3), mark as "not now," recycle in 60–90 days. Never burn a bridge — these owners refer each other.
- Every call: log time, outcome, exact objection verbatim. Objections feed the handlers in Section 1.
